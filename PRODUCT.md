# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary: recruiters and hiring managers worldwide**, usually arriving from a resume, LinkedIn, or a job application. They scan for 30–90 seconds: role, stack, proof of shipped work, how to contact. Many will open it on a phone.
- **Secondary: other developers, peers, and potential freelance clients** who stay longer and explore the craft.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Sankalp Shrivastav, a full-stack developer based in India who is open to work. It should get Sankalp hired by proving two things at once: he ships real full-stack products, and he has the taste and frontend craft to make something memorable. Success means a recruiter leaves knowing his role, his stack, two or three real projects, and how to reach him, and remembers the site.

## Positioning

A story told as a space mission: the visitor follows one journey through sections instead of reading a brochure. The site itself is the strongest frontend project in the portfolio.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page Next.js 16 (App Router) + React 19 + Tailwind v4 site. Animation libraries already installed: GSAP 3, Motion (framer-motion), OGL (WebGL).
- Every section is animated and scroll-driven, but content must stay readable without animation (reduced motion) and on slow mobile devices.
- All personal content lives in one editable data file so Sankalp can update it without touching components.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Sankalp Shrivastav. Handle: "Sankalp-Space" / `sankalp_spacex`; the space theme is his own identity, not a gimmick.
- Theme pinned by the user: space, astronaut, planets. Gen Z energy, award-site ambition (Awwwards-class), "a living thing, not a dull portfolio".
- Voice from his GitHub README: casual, self-aware humour ("I write better code at 2 AM than I do at 10 AM. My console has seen more drama than Netflix").

## Product Principles

1. Proof over adjectives: every claim points to a shipped project or link.
2. Thirty-second recruiter path: role, stack, best work and contact are reachable without finishing the story.
3. The experience is the portfolio piece; motion carries meaning (the journey), never noise.
4. Content is data: Sankalp edits one file, the story updates.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Worldwide audience: plain English, WCAG AA contrast on the dark theme, full keyboard navigation, `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured with a static but still beautiful version.
